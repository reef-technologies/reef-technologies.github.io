Your choice

Optional analytics and Google contact form protection are off until you accept them. You can select them separately using , or reject both. You can still browse the site and contact us by email. Withdrawal stops future optional collection; it does not undo processing that already occurred.

Analytics

With your consent, Counter.dev receives page paths, referring pages and screen dimensions to help us understand use of the website. Its servers receive connection information such as your IP address. See Counter.dev privacy information.

Contact form

When you enable contact form protection and send a message, Google reCAPTCHA processes browser and device information for spam detection. Google may set cookies on its own domain, which this website cannot delete. Withdrawing consent prevents this site from loading it again. See Google’s privacy policy and terms.

The name, email address, subject and message you submit are sent through Getform so we can receive and respond to your enquiry. We process enquiries to take steps you request before a contract, or for our legitimate interest in responding to business correspondence. Do not include sensitive information. See Getform’s privacy policy. You can instead email us directly without enabling Google protection.

Other requests and your rights

Hosting and asset providers receive connection information needed to deliver the site. Fonts are delivered by Google Fonts. Third-party providers may process information outside the European Economic Area; their policies describe processing locations and safeguards.

We retain correspondence for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and meet applicable legal obligations. Contact us for details of applicable retention and transfer arrangements, or to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability. You may complain to the Polish data protection authority, UODO. Consent to optional tracking can be withdrawn at any time through Cookie settings.
